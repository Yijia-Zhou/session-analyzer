The reusable `busyButton` style reserves a small indicator beside the unchanged label. `aria-busy` controls the animation, while `aria-disabled` and the action handler suppress activation without discarding keyboard focus. A separate feedback owner survives replacement of the resolved evidence object; cancellation and owner-checked finalization prevent stale actions from changing a newer indicator. Repeat pointer/touch activation and Enter/Space on the busy control are ignored; scrolling or other navigation can still cancel. / 可复用 busyButton 样式在不变的文字旁预留小型标记位置。aria-busy 控制动画；aria-disabled 与操作处理函数抑制触发而不丢弃键盘焦点。独立反馈 owner 在已解析证据对象被替换时继续存在；取消及核验 owner 的结束处理避免旧操作改变新等待状态。同一忙碌控件上的重复指针／触摸触发及 Enter／空格会被忽略，滚动及其他导航仍可取消。
